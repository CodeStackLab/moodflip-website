Overall page design direction
Use the same peaceful sunrise/sun background style across the main pages: About, Privacy Policy, Contact, Login and other simple information pages. Keep the background very soft/faded so the text stays easy to read. Put page text inside clean white or warm-cream content cards.

     About page


About MoodFlip
MoodFlip helps you notice your current mood, understand the feeling behind it, and make one step toward feeling better.
You start by choosing your current mood, then a more specific feeling. MoodFlip then suggests a better-feeling and a short 60-second action that you can try straight away.
MoodFlip is designed for small emotional shifts - not big promises. It is not therapy, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support.
Our aim is simple:
Notice your mood.
Name the feeling behind your mood.
Take one step toward feeling better.
You can use the basic MoodFlip tool without creating a profile. If you choose to create a profile, you can save check-ins and create personalised mood reports.
Your mood does not have to stay where it is. MoodFlip helps you notice what you feel, shows you a direction to feel better, and you can take one small action toward it.

     Privacy Policy page


Privacy Policy
Last updated: 21 August 2026
MoodFlip respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect and how it is used.
1. Using MoodFlip without a profile
You can use the basic MoodFlip tool without creating a profile.
If you do not create a profile, MoodFlip does not need to store your email address or saved mood history.
2. Information we may collect
If you create a profile, MoodFlip may store:
• your email address
• selected moods and feelings
• check-in dates
• actions shown to you
• purchase history for paid downloads
This information is used to provide saved check-ins, personalised downloads, and relevant MoodFlip offers.
3. Paid downloads
If you buy a paid MoodFlip download, payment may be processed through a secure third-party payment provider.
MoodFlip does not need to store your full card details.
4. 90-day automatic deletion
Inactive profiles and saved mood history are automatically deleted after 90 days.
This helps keep stored information limited and relevant.
5. Email messages
If you create a profile, you may receive emails related to your saved check-ins, paid downloads, reminders, or relevant MoodFlip offers.
You can unsubscribe from marketing emails.
6. Not medical or crisis support
MoodFlip is a self-reflection tool. It is not therapy, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support.
If you feel unsafe or need urgent help, please contact emergency services or a crisis support service in your country.


     Developer notes

Background and page styling notes
• Use the peaceful sunrise/sun background style on all simple information pages.
• Keep the background soft and faded so the text is never hard to read.
• Use white or warm-cream content cards over the background.
• Use rounded corners and soft shadow on content cards to match the homepage feel.
• Do not make the background busy behind Privacy Policy paragraphs.
• Keep the MoodFlip logo in the top-left header, without TM.
• Keep the same top navigation order: Home | About | Privacy Policy | Contact | Login.
• No affiliate links at launch unless Joy approves them separately.
